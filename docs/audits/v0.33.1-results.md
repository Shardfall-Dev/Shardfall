# Shardfall v0.33.1 — stabilization results (2026-11-28)

Input: the verified audit `docs/audits/v0.33-stability-2026-11-28.md` (branch `audit/v0.33-stability-2026-11-28`, 98e88c9), baseline main fba88cc (PR #47).
Scope: stabilization only. No gameplay, balance, AI or content changes; guard AI untouched; no test weakened or skipped. Saves stay format 9; the new fields are optional and ignored by older builds. Build: `./build.sh 0.33.1` → `Shardfall_v0.33.1.html` (the title and file name carry the release, as v0.28.2 did).

## 1. Intermittent target-frame combat check (audit P1) — reproduced, fixed at the cause

Check: `tests/ui-smoke/run.py`, "no attack after closing; the creature keeps its own fight (unchanged AI)". CI run 46073533894 failed it once; the same SHA passed in the Pages run.

**Reproduction.** Twelve runs of the unmodified suite against the v0.33 build (two and three in parallel) did not fail it; a diagnostic copy then logged every `ActorDamaged` on the boar, the boar's state and target, the player's health and every actor within 12 tiles:

- At that point of the suite the player stands at (122.5, 304.5) with 31 of 72 health, left over from the earlier random creature fights. Health is never restored before the block.
- Two Saltmarsh quay wardens stand 6 and 9 tiles away, but they have no fighting profile (`npcFight` absent), so they never hit the boar. No other source damaged it in any run: guards were not the cause on that spot, but nothing kept them out of the fixture.
- The armed player (unarmed template, boar at 1.2 tiles) never swung in the gap between the health snapshot and the click, even with 1.5 s of extra real time.
- Continuing the run past the 40 steps showed the boar killing the player at about game second 46 (health 13 → 1 → ghost). A dead player clears the boar's target and it goes idle.
- Forcing the player's health to 3 before the click reproduces the exact CI failure ("no attack after closing; the creature keeps its own fight"); the later "Escape 3" check then fails too, because the player is a ghost.

**Root cause.** The fixture did not control the player's health (random after the preceding fights) or time (the real `requestAnimationFrame` loop keeps stepping the simulation between Playwright calls, more on a slow runner), so the boar could kill the player inside the window and end its own fight. The check also assumed that only the player could change the boar's health, while guards (`src/npcfight.js`) and creature AI (`src/game.js` `aiMon`/`ecoThink`) may legitimately damage or retarget it.

**Fix (test only; no game code changed for this).**
- The block runs on clear ground found at run time: standable, outside protected ground, with no NPC, creature, pet or ally within 30 tiles; the player is at full health.
- Every `ActorDamaged` is recorded with its source and event sequence number. After the close: no damage from the player, no new player attack clip, the boar's health unchanged since the close, and no damage from anyone else (there is nobody else).
- The boar's own AI is a separate check: it still targets the player and is in chase / telegraph / strike / recover, and the player is alive.
- **Projectiles (explicit decision):** an arrow released before the close may still land; that is the committed-release rule in `arrowLands` (one second of flight). New check: with a bow, one arrow in flight at the close, at most one hit from the player afterwards, and no further arrow is spent or released.
- **Independent guard AI:** a Vespar guard placed 7 tiles away, just outside protected ground and inside his jurisdiction, still steps in after the player closes the frame, and every hit on the boar after the close is his; none is the player's. Guard behaviour is unchanged.

Three other harness races were found while repeating the suites and in CI, and fixed the same way (no assertion relaxed):
- "No tooltip before the hover delay" failed once under heavy load (three suites in parallel): the 120 ms Playwright wait plus a round trip exceeded the 300 ms delay. The delay is now measured in the page, from the pointer entering the tile to the moment `#itip` appears; it must be ≥ 295 ms (setTimeout never fires early).
- sfx-smoke "holding against a wall: no footsteps" failed in the push run 46109369280 (commit 97e6be2): the player did not move and played no footstep, but a nearby rat's `rat.alert` was in the list of sounds and the check counted every sound. It now counts footsteps (`step.*`) only, which is what it is named for; the position check is unchanged.
- "Armor popup" failed in 1 of 16 and 1 of 10 runs: the bandage popup opened beside its tile can cover the tunic tile (a diagnostic showed the popup's close button at the tunic's centre); Playwright's retry with other scroll positions then raced the virtualized grid's redraw. The test now checks that the grid shows both split stacks, closes the popup with Escape (checked), then clicks the tunic.

**Repeats.** Earlier versions of the new fixture: 16 + 10 complete runs, no combat-close failure. Final suite: **22 of 22 complete runs fully green** (two in parallel), each including the combat-close, AI, arrow and guard checks.

## 4. Pet Stay position and identity (audit P2)

Before: `save()` stored only offsets from the player; boot snapped any pet 12+ tiles away beside the player, even with order Stay, and pets got a new actor id on every load.

Now (`src/game.js`):
- The save also keeps each pet's `x`, `y`, `aid` (actor id) and `owner` (character id); `dx`/`dy` remain for older builds.
- `petLoadPos`: a staying pet appears at its saved position, or for older saves at the saved character position plus the saved offset (the same point). The spot must be on the map and standable; otherwise it falls back beside the player as before. Following pets behave exactly as before.
- `idKey`: a loaded pet claims its saved actor id; if the id is taken it gets a new one as before.
- Loyalty, feeding time, name, slots and order are restored as before.
- Not changed: the follow teleport beyond 24 tiles and the direct (non-navigator) pet movement in `petTick`. Changing them is new behaviour, not stabilization.

## 5. Character creation when browser storage fails (audit P1)

Verified before the fix on the v0.33 build: with `setItem` failing, `__CC.quick` returned true, nothing was stored and nothing on screen said so beyond one log line.

Now:
- `save()` is split into `saveData()` (the save object) and the write. A failed write sets `SAVE.unsaved` and shows the **Not saved** notice (`#unsaved`, 44 px buttons) with **Export** (the current game as `Shardfall_<name>_unsaved.json`, built without writing storage) and **Retry**. Each autosave retries; success hides the notice and logs "Your game is saved again".
- `boot()` logs that a new character has not been saved and that the previous save is unchanged. The game still runs, so nothing typed into the creator is lost.
- `ccCommit()`: when replacing a save, the copy of the old save (`.replaced` or `.damaged`) must be written first; if it cannot be, creation stops with a message and nothing changes. If the copy works but the new save is refused, the old save stays the stored one.
- Storage blocked entirely (every `localStorage` access throws, as in some private modes): the creator, the title screen and the game all keep working; the notice stays up; no page errors. The title-screen export no longer throws there.

## 6. Stale tabs and deleted characters (audit P1)

Verified before the fix: a tab kept running after the save was deleted in another tab, and its next autosave wrote the deleted character back.

Now:
- `SAVE.expect` holds the stored save this tab last read (`bootSaved`) or wrote. Before every write the stored value is compared with it; if another tab deleted or replaced it, this tab stops saving and says why. This also covers a storage event that never arrived.
- The `storage` handler also reacts to deletion (`newValue` null) and to `localStorage.clear()` (key null).
- The existing two-tab behaviour (the older tab stops when the newer writes) is unchanged.
